The popup, on an AP Classroom quiz page.

Export to text

Pick your course, hit extract, and download the multiple-choice set as a .txt you can study anywhere. Image-based choices get flagged instead of dropped silently.

Answer Hider

Covers the correct and incorrect indicators so a review page works as practice. Toggle it from the popup or the floating panel on the page.

AI study tools

Generate similar questions, flashcards, or a concept summary from what you extracted. Bring your own Claude API key; it stays in your browser.
